FAQ

Questions?

What is BizaroHQ?
BizaroHQ is a suite of business management applications including BizBay (garage), BizStay (hotel/PG), BizBite (POS), and BizNote (notes) available on web, iOS, and Android.
Do I need internet to use BizaroHQ?
No! All products work 100% offline. Data syncs automatically when your connection returns, ensuring zero downtime.
Is BizaroHQ GST compliant?
Yes, completely! Automated GST calculations, GSTR reports, and e-invoicing support included in all products.
Can I use BizaroHQ on multiple devices?
Yes! Use on web, iPhone, iPad, or Android. Your data syncs across all devices in real-time.
What is the refund policy?
No long-term contracts. Cancel anytime. Your money back if you're not satisfied in the first 30 days.
Do you provide customer support?
Yes! 24/7 support via phone, email, WhatsApp, and live chat in your language.
